Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

🔒

Who we are

Vote Saathi is a website by Mohammad Asif that makes voting information simple, safe and accessible for everyone.

What information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell your personal information. You do not need an account to use this website.

How the features work

⚠️

Scam Detector

The message you paste is checked inside your own browser. It is not sent to us or stored anywhere.

🧠

Quiz

Your answers and score stay on your screen. We do not save them.

🙋

Helper Pledge

The name you type is only shown back to you on the page. It is not saved.

🔊

Easy Mode

Your choice is remembered on your own device so the setting stays on between pages. You can clear it by clearing your browser data.

Links to other websites

We link to official websites such as the Election Commission of India. We do not control those sites, and their privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Hosting

This website is hosted by a third-party service, which may keep standard technical logs such as visit times and browser type.

Children

This website is meant to be safe for all ages. We do not ask for personal details from anyone.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
