Learn About Voting

Voting is your right. Here are the steps, from registering to casting your vote.

🗳️

5 simple steps

1

Check eligibility

You must be an Indian citizen aged 18 or above.

2

Register

Apply as a new voter using Form 6 on the official Voters' Service Portal or the Voter Helpline App.

3

Check your name

Make sure your name is in the electoral roll for your area.

4

Carry your ID

Take your voter ID (EPIC) or another approved photo ID.

5

Cast your vote

Get your finger inked, press the button on the EVM, and check the VVPAT slip.

Common questions

What is NOTA?

NOTA means "None Of The Above". It lets you vote without choosing any candidate.

Is my vote secret?

Yes. Nobody can ask who you voted for, and you don't have to tell anyone.

Does registering cost money?

No. Voter registration is free. Anyone asking for money is a scammer.

Who can I call for help?

The Voter Helpline number is 1950.
